# Image And Asset Direction

## Asset Principles
- Use the official ECOTUCA/Perifa logo assets without distortion or recolor.
- Prefer real territorial/educational imagery with consent and credits.
- Avoid poverty, violence, police operation or exploitative imagery.
- Avoid generic corporate blue/purple stock visuals.
- Use brand color fields, pattern strips, editorial photography and UI screenshots.

## Required Production Assets
- ECOTUCA horizontal logo in SVG.
- ECOTUCA vertical logo in SVG.
- ECOTUCA symbol in SVG.
- Perifa Ecotour signature in SVG.
- Troika licensed font files.
- Lufga licensed font files.
- 6 to 10 real approved photos for public pages.
- Teacher/student/classroom or workshop imagery with consent.
- Course thumbnail system.
- Certificate visual template.

## Generated Image Prompt Direction
Use generation only for abstract/editorial support assets, not to fake real community documentation.

Sophisticated dashboard background texture inspired by community education and environmental impact, dark brown base, subtle yellow and mint accents, no charts with fake numbers, no text, no logos.

## Screen Visual Direction
- Public home: real brand and emotional clarity.
- Student dashboard: welcoming and motivating.
- Lesson page: focused, calm, minimal distraction.
- Teacher dashboard: operational and warm.
- Admin dashboard: dense, serious, premium.

## Do Not Generate
- Fake official logos.
- Fake photos of identifiable students/community members.
- Fake certificates implying real completion.
- Fake partner logos.
- Fake maps with precise meeting points.

## Production Review
All final images should be reviewed by Bruno/equipe before launch and credited in `docs/media` or the production CMS.
