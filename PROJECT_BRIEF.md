# Project Brief

## Product
ECOTUCA / Perifa Ecotour is a digital platform that combines an ecotourism school, a guided route marketplace and an impact reporting layer for the Complexo do Alemao.

## Objective
Create a navigable MVP for an important presentation. The MVP must look and feel like a real product even where data is mocked. It must demonstrate education, territory, youth professional training, sustainable tourism, Agenda 2030 and income generation.

## Audience
- Youth from the territory joining ecotourism training.
- Teachers and project facilitators.
- Guides formed by ECOTUCA.
- Tourists interested in community-based ecological routes.
- Project coordination, UNISUAM/Favela Art and funders needing accountability data.

## Core Modules
1. Access, registration and manual approval.
2. Student learning area with tracks, lessons, progress, certificates and community.
3. Teacher area with classes, content and forum support.
4. Administrative panel with approvals, metrics and reports.
5. Mobile app showcase for routes, guides, bookings and tourist interest.
